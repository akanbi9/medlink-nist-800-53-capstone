# SA — System and Services Acquisition

## Purpose

The System and Services Acquisition (SA) family defines security requirements for acquiring, developing, or subscribing to technology and services.

The purpose is to ensure that security requirements are considered before new systems or services are approved.

## Practical Evidence

This folder contains:

1. Security Requirements Checklist
2. Vendor Due-Diligence Questionnaire
3. Contract Security Clauses
4. Secure Acquisition Acceptance Criteria

## Assessment Status

**Status:** Partially Implemented — Simulated

The assessment scenario indicates that security requirements are not consistently included in contracts for new cloud services. Standardized acquisition and vendor-security evidence was not provided in the scenario.

## Key Improvement

MedLink Services Ltd. should establish a standardized acquisition process that includes:

- Security requirements
- Vendor due diligence
- Contract security requirements
- Security acceptance criteria
- Final security approval

  # Contract Security Clauses

The following security requirements should be included in applicable contracts with technology and service providers.

## 1. Confidentiality

The vendor shall protect MedLink information from unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or use.

## 2. Access Control

Vendor access shall be limited to authorized personnel who require access to perform the agreed services.

## 3. Authentication

Administrative and remote access should use strong authentication, including MFA where supported and required.

## 4. Data Protection

The vendor shall protect MedLink data and shall only use the data for the agreed business purpose.

## 5. Encryption

Sensitive information shall be protected using appropriate encryption during transmission and, where applicable, while stored.

## 6. Incident Notification

The vendor shall notify MedLink of security incidents affecting MedLink systems or data within the agreed timeframe.

## 7. Logging and Monitoring

The vendor shall maintain appropriate security logs and provide relevant information when required for security investigations.

## 8. Backup and Recovery

Where applicable, the vendor shall maintain appropriate backup and recovery measures.

## 9. Vulnerability Management

The vendor shall maintain processes for identifying and addressing security vulnerabilities affecting the service.

## 10. Data Retention and Deletion

At the end of the service, MedLink data shall be returned or securely deleted according to the agreed requirements.

## 11. Third-Party Access

The vendor shall identify relevant subcontractors or third parties that may access or process MedLink information.

## 12. Termination and Offboarding

Vendor access shall be removed when the service is terminated, and required data shall be returned or securely deleted.
