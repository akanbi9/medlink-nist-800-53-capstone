# MP — Removable Media Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes rules for the use, storage, transportation,
and disposal of removable media containing organizational information.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- USB flash drives
- External hard drives
- Memory cards
- CDs/DVDs
- Backup media
- Printed sensitive documents

## 3. Authorized Use

Only organization-approved removable media may be connected to
company systems.

Users must not connect unknown or personally owned USB devices
to organizational computers without authorization.

## 4. Sensitive Information

Sensitive or confidential information should only be stored on
approved and protected media.

Where appropriate, sensitive data must be encrypted.

## 5. Transportation

Removable media containing sensitive information must be protected
during transportation and must not be left unattended.

## 6. Storage

Unused removable media must be stored in a secure location with
access limited to authorized personnel.

## 7. Malware Protection

Removable media must be scanned for malware before being used
on organizational systems.

## 8. Reuse

Before removable media is assigned to another user, organizational
information must be securely removed according to the sanitization
procedure.

## 9. Disposal

Media containing sensitive information must be securely sanitized
or physically destroyed before disposal.

## 10. Prohibited Activities

Users must not:
- Use unauthorized USB drives.
- Copy sensitive information without authorization.
- Leave sensitive media unattended.
- Share organizational media with unauthorized persons.
- Dispose of sensitive media in normal waste.
