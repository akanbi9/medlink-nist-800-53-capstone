# Audit-Log Review Procedure

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this procedure is to ensure that security and audit logs are regularly reviewed to identify unauthorized access, suspicious activities, security incidents, and policy violations.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to logs generated by Microsoft 365, endpoints, firewalls, the customer portal, HR systems, finance systems, file servers, and VPN services.

## 3. Review Frequency

Security logs shall be reviewed at least weekly by the responsible IT, network, application, HR, or finance administrator.

Critical security alerts should be reviewed immediately when detected.

## 4. Review Process

The reviewer shall:

1. Collect audit logs from the approved log sources.
2. Check for repeated failed login attempts.
3. Check for logins from unusual locations or devices.
4. Review administrator account activities.
5. Check for unauthorized access attempts.
6. Review firewall traffic for suspicious connections.
7. Check for unusual file access, modification, or deletion.
8. Review malware and endpoint security alerts.
9. Compare suspicious activities with known users and approved activities.
10. Record significant findings in the weekly log-review record.
11. Escalate suspicious or confirmed security events according to the escalation criteria.
12. Store the review record securely for future reference.

## 5. Evidence

The following evidence should be maintained:

* Audit logs
* Weekly log-review records
* Security alerts
* Incident reports
* Screenshots of relevant security events
* Records of escalated incidents

## 6. Retention

Audit logs shall normally be retained for 12 months unless a longer retention period is required by organizational policy, legal requirements, or an active investigation.

## Escalation Criteria
# Escalation Criteria

Suspicious or potentially malicious events shall be escalated when they meet one or more of the following criteria.

| Event                                                   | Severity | Escalation Action                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Multiple failed login attempts                          | Medium   | Notify IT Administrator and investigate                                |
| Successful login after many failed attempts             | High     | Investigate immediately and verify the account owner                   |
| Login from an unusual location                          | Medium   | Verify with the user and review account activity                       |
| Suspected compromised administrator account             | Critical | Immediately notify IT/Security Lead and disable or protect the account |
| Malware detected on an endpoint                         | High     | Isolate the affected endpoint and notify IT/Security Lead              |
| Unauthorized access to customer data                    | Critical | Immediately escalate and initiate incident-response procedures         |
| Large or unusual file transfer                          | High     | Investigate and escalate if unauthorized                               |
| Unauthorized firewall configuration change              | High     | Investigate and notify the Network Administrator                       |
| Repeated suspicious network connections                 | High     | Investigate source and destination and escalate when necessary         |
| Unauthorized modification or deletion of important logs | Critical | Immediately escalate to the IT/Security Lead                           |
| Suspicious USB/removable-media activity                 | High     | Disconnect or isolate the device and investigate                       |
| Confirmed account compromise                            | Critical | Escalate immediately and begin incident-response procedures            |

## Escalation Levels

### Low

Normal activity or a minor event requiring documentation.

**Action:** Record and close after review.

### Medium

Suspicious activity that requires investigation.

**Action:** Investigate and notify the responsible administrator.

### High

Likely security incident or activity that could cause significant harm.

**Action:** Immediately investigate and escalate to the IT/Security Lead.

### Critical

Confirmed or highly suspected compromise, data breach, or major security incident.

**Action:** Immediately escalate and activate the organization's incident-response process.
