# Visitor Access Procedure
**on how visitors are controlled when they enter the organization.**
Visitor arrives
      ↓
Reception verifies identity
      ↓
Purpose of visit recorded
      ↓
Host/employee confirms visit
      ↓
Visitor receives visitor badge
      ↓
Visitor is escorted where required
      ↓
Visitor enters approved area
      ↓
Visit completed
      ↓
Badge returned
      ↓
Visitor signs out

# Environmental Risk Table

| Risk               | Possible Impact              | Existing Control      | Risk Level  | Recommended Action        |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------- | --------------------- | ----------- | ------------------------- |
| Power failure      | Systems shut down            | UPS                   | High        | Add backup power          |
| Power surge        | Equipment damage             | Surge protection      | High        | Test protection regularly |
| Heat               | Server overheating           | Air conditioning      | High        | Monitor temperature       |
| Fire               | Equipment/data destruction   | Fire extinguisher     | Critical    | Add smoke detection       |
| Water leak         | Equipment damage             | Equipment elevated    | High        | Inspect plumbing          |
| Theft              | Loss of equipment/data       | CCTV + access control | High        | Improve physical security |
| Unauthorized entry | Equipment/data compromise    | Door locks            | High        | Add access control        |
| Flooding           | Equipment damage             | Elevated equipment    | Medium/High | Improve drainage          |
| Dust               | Equipment overheating/damage | Regular cleaning      | Medium      | Schedule cleaning         |
